An online grocer is either a brick-and-mortar supermarket or grocery store that allows online ordering, or a
standalone e-commerce service that includes grocery items.There is usually a delivery charge for this service.
Brick-and-mortar supermarkets that have built internet channels to better service their clients are
known as online grocers.[3] Online grocery delivery services are available throughout Europe, Asia and North America,
mostly in urban centers. The online ordering is done through e-commerce websites or mobile apps.
